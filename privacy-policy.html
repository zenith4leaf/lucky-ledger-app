Lucky Ledger Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026.10.05

zenith.4leaf (“we”) respects the privacy of everyone who uses the Lucky Ledger app (the “App”). Your ledger data is stored only on your device by default. We do not collect it on our own servers or view it.

3. Sharing with third parties and service providers

We do not sell or share your information with third parties. When you turn on the features below, the provider’s service processes the related data.

ProviderWhat it processesWhen
Google LLC (Google Sign-In, Google Drive)Account authentication, storing backup and shared filesWhen you sign in with Google
Google LLC / Apple Inc. (device speech recognition)Converting speech to text; depending on device settings, audio may be sent to their serversWhen you use voice input
Google LLC (ML Kit text recognition)Reading text on receipt photos, processed on the deviceWhen you use receipt input
  • Drive backups are saved in a hidden app-only folder in your own Drive. We cannot access them.
  • With family sharing on, only the accounts the admin assigns, plus shared accounts, are shared with invited family members. Shared files are encrypted with a family key (AES-256).
  • If we add ads (such as Google AdMob) or paid features (Google Play or App Store in-app purchases), we will update this policy with the data and providers involved before they go live.

4. Use of Google user data

The App uses Google Drive access only to back up and restore your ledger and to create and manage family sharing folders. It does not read or change Drive files the App did not create. The App’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This data is not used for advertising or AI training and is not transferred to others.

제4조 (구글 사용자 데이터의 사용)

앱은 구글 드라이브 권한을 백업·복원과 가족 공유 폴더 생성·권한 관리에만 사용합니다. 앱이 만들지 않은 다른 드라이브 파일은 읽거나 바꾸지 않습니다. 구글 API로 받은 정보의 사용은 Google API 서비스 사용자 데이터 정책의 제한적 사용(Limited Use) 요건을 따르며, 광고나 AI 학습에 쓰지 않고 제3자에게 넘기지 않습니다.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device is deleted immediately when you uninstall the App or use Settings › Reset data.
  • Only the 10 most recent Drive backups are kept; older ones are deleted automatically. To remove all of them, open Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps and choose “Delete hidden app data” for Lucky Ledger.
  • Family sharing files stay in the admin’s Drive after sharing stops. The admin can delete them in Drive.
  • To disconnect Google, sign out in the App or remove Lucky Ledger under Google Account › Security › Third-party connections.

제5조 (보관 기간과 파기)

  • 기기에 저장된 정보는 앱 삭제, 또는 설정 › 데이터 초기화 시 즉시 지워집니다.
  • 드라이브 백업은 최근 10개만 남기고 오래된 것은 자동으로 지웁니다. 전부 지우려면 구글 드라이브 › 설정 › 앱 관리에서 행운가계부의 “숨김 앱 데이터 삭제”를 선택하세요.
  • 가족 공유 파일은 공유를 중지해도 관리자의 드라이브에 남으며, 관리자가 드라이브에서 직접 지울 수 있습니다.
  • 구글 연결을 끊으려면 앱에서 로그아웃하거나 구글 계정 › 보안 › 타사 앱 액세스에서 행운가계부의 접근을 삭제하세요.

You can view, edit, delete and export your records to Excel inside the App at any time. Because we do not hold your data, no separate access or deletion request is needed. You can still contact us with any question.

7. Security

  • Your App PIN is hashed and kept in the device’s secure storage.
  • Family sharing files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The admin’s recovery key is locked again with the App PIN.
  • All network traffic uses Google’s encrypted connections (HTTPS).

제7조 (안전성 확보 조치)

  • 앱 PIN은 해시되어 기기 보안 저장소에 보관됩니다.
  • 가족 공유 파일은 AES-256-GCM으로 암호화되며, 관리자 복구용 키는 앱 PIN으로 다시 잠깁니다.
  • 통신은 모두 구글의 암호화 연결(HTTPS)을 사용합니다.
